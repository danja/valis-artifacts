# Brief

Make a song in the style of The Doors' "Riders on the Storm", as an end-to-end test of Valis: the sampler drum machine,
sounds fetched from freesound.org over MCP, and the song-writing pipeline.

"In the style of" is taken as the mood and the instruments, not a tune: no melody, chord sequence or lyric of the
original is used. What is borrowed is a slow, loping jazz-rock feel; a minor mode with a vamp (E Dorian); a Rhodes-style
electric piano and a soft electric bass; a light kit played mostly on the ride; one clean, echoing guitar; and a
storm on the soundtrack. The piece is an instrumental. The original has a whispered vocal; this has none.
