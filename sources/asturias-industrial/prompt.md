# Brief

Make an industrial dubstep version of Isaac Albéniz's "Asturias (Leyenda)": 140 bpm in E minor, heavy and
mechanical, with drops, that quotes the original's arpeggio.

"Asturias" is taken as the harmony and the arpeggio figure, not a transcription: the chord loop `Em B7 Em C B7 Em`
and a top-note line that traces the tremolo. "Industrial dubstep" is taken as the palette: a four-to-the-floor
kick, a wobble bass on the half-time drop, a 909 kit, metal percussion, a fuzz lead, and long risers.

Everything is reported as measured, not heard.
