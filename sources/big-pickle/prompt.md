# Brief

Make a song called "Big Pickle": driving techno, with a couple of risers and drops, that conjures up the
year 2000's view of the future.

"Driving techno" is taken as a fast four-to-the-floor pulse, a rolling bass, offbeat chord stabs and a
build into each drop. "The year 2000's view of the future" is taken as the palette rather than a quotation:
the bright digital arpeggio, the wide detuned saw lead, the supersaw chord, the long reverb tail, the
first-generation ringtone bleep, and a machine waking up as the piece starts. No melody, chord sequence or
sample of an existing record is used. The piece is an instrumental.

Everything is reported as measured, not heard. Where a balance or a character is still unheard, the track
README says so.

Built by [`../../scripts/big-pickle/make.py`](../../scripts/big-pickle/make.py). The circuits, the score and
the layout are all written by that one script.
