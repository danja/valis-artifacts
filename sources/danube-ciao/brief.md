# Danube Ciao

A 3/4 folk-orchestral waltz that combines Johann Strauss II's public-domain
"The Blue Danube" with the traditional Italian song "Bella Ciao". The Danube
figure stays in D major; Bella Ciao is transposed from G minor to B minor, the
relative minor of D. A bass note on beat one and clipped chords on beats two
and three make the same lilting pulse carry both tunes.

The arrangement moves from one theme to the other, then brings the melodies
together over a shared D-major/B-minor harmony. The score uses short, newly
arranged excerpts rather than a full transcription of either work.
