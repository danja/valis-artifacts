# All Fall Down

A 140 bpm dance track in A minor, 104 bars (3:00), whose tunes are nursery rhymes moved onto the minor scale. Each tune
stays recognisable and sounds eerie, and the structure is the ordinary one: intro, build, drop, break, build, drop, outro.
The tunes are traditional and out of copyright.

* "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star" (also "Baa, Baa, Black Sheep"; the tune is the French song "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman", 1761)
  is the main hook. The lead plays it. In the second drop the bass plays it two octaves down, and the bass circuit says "baa" on each note.
* "London Bridge Is Falling Down" is the break and the outro, the falling lullaby the track ends on.
* "Mary Had a Little Lamb" opens the track on the toy-piano pluck, and returns at the very end, so the track ends where it began.

The first build gives the first four bars of the star tune as a promise (the lead enters at bar 5), then the first drop
plays all eight. The tune's last note is the second degree, B, so each time round it asks a question and the loop
answers it. The eight-bar chord loop is Am F Dm E, F G Am E.

The eight instruments are `../../../valis/examples/nursery-*.ttl` (copied to `circuits/`), described in the Valis repository
under `docs/examples.md` and built by its `scripts/nursery/nursery.py`: Humpty Kick, Rattle Snare, Ding Dong Hat, Black Sheep Bass,
Twinkle Lead, Incy Wincy Arp, Rock-a-bye Pad and Wind-up Riser, plus a reverb return on the lead.

Structure: intro 16 bars, build 8, **drop** 16, break 16, build 8, **drop** 24 (the bass takes the tune in bars 1 to 8 and 17 to 24),
outro 16. A build ends in a snare roll and a gap, then the drop.

* `mixes/all-fall-down-v3.flac`: the mix, made with `valis-song mix --arc`. Integrated loudness -11.0 LUFS (the target was
  -10; the true-peak ceiling of -1 dBTP set the gain), loudness range 11.5 LU, section levels: intro -20.6, drops -13.1 and -11.6,
  break -17.5, outro -15.1. **Measured, not heard.** Versions 1 and 2 are kept as WAV and are not in git. In v1 the snare and hat followed the kick and were 25 and 45 dB
  too quiet (see below). In v2 they were levelled alone, but the quiet sections were low (loudness range 16.1 LU, -11.2 LUFS).
  v3 raised the intro, break and outro, the lead by 3 dB and the hat by 2 dB.
* `song.ttl`, `score.ttl`: the layout and the notes as data. Both are generated by `../../scripts/all-fall-down/make.py`
  from its table of sections and plays; change the table and run it again.
* `midi/`: kick, snare, hat, bass, lead, arp, pad, riser (written by `valis-song score`).
* `all-fall-down-midi.rpp`: a Reaper project with a track for each part holding its MIDI and a Valis instrument already loaded
  with the part's circuit and its MCP server switched on, at ports 7677 and up in track order (the same as the order above).
* `wavs/`: the stems (ignored by git; rebuild with `valis-song render song.ttl`).

Rebuild: `python3 ../../scripts/all-fall-down/make.py`, then `valis-song score`, `render`, `stems`, `mix song.ttl --version vN --arc`.

Notes from making it:

* `val:follows` takes an offset in dB from the leader's gain, and the level cannot be above 0, which is not what the Valis docs said.
  The first mix set the snare and hat to follow the kick with their target levels, which put them at the kick's gain less 19 and 27 dB. The snare was 25 dB
  and the hat 45 dB under the kick in the drops. They are levelled alone now, by their own playing rms.
* In the drops (v3) the kick is at -17 dB rms, bass -21 to -23, lead -24 to -21, arp and pad -26, snare -24, hat -29 (measured over the
  section including rests).
* The lead stem sits at its limiter on the loudest notes (peak -1.0 dBFS); it is not clipped, but it is not loose either.
* The listening is still to do: whether the star tune is the earworm (it is the simplest tune in the track, played in unison
  in the lead and, in the second drop, the bass), whether the toy-piano arp's octave-up echoes blur the tune in the intro, whether the hat is too
  bright (its spectral centroid is 12.7 kHz), and the transition from the break into the second build.
