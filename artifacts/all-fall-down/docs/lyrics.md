A short, broken text. The voice starts calm, as if reciting a rhyme to a child, and comes apart as the track goes on: lines repeat, words stick, the rhymes run into each other and lose their order, and at the end the voice is calm again but has forgotten how the rhyme goes. The fragments are traditional nursery rhymes, which are out of copyright. Each word is spoken on a beat (a bar at 140 bpm is 1.714 seconds); `_` is a beat of rest.
