# valis-artifacts

Things made by Valis.

[Valis](https://github.com/valis) (Virtual Analog LLM Integrated System) is a standalone app and VST plugin that builds virtual-analog
instruments and effects from circuits described in RDF/Turtle. Each piece here is written as a score, played by
Valis circuits, rendered to one stem per part and mixed. The MIDI files and the score script are kept, and the
stems can be rebuilt from them, so that a piece can be finished in a DAW.

## Artifacts

| Track | Description |
|---|---|
| [Autumn Leaves (acid rock)](artifacts/autumn-leaves/README.md) | About five minutes of psychedelic acid rock on the chord changes of the standard: hypnotic drums, a fuzz bass, a tonewheel organ through a rotating speaker, horn stabs and a fuzz lead with space left for a guitar. Mix: [`mixes/autumn-leaves.flac`](artifacts/autumn-leaves/mixes/autumn-leaves.flac) (24-bit FLAC). |
| [Lights On, Nobody Home (Latin punk)](artifacts/lights-on-nobody-home/README.md) | A three-minute song for nylon guitars, guitarron, harp, trumpets and hand percussion. Bright mariachi verses in C major about people who are not very bright, then a chorus that jumps into D Hungarian minor and turns punk, with a chanted "blank, blank, blank, blank". Lyrics and a note-by-note vocal guide included, vocals to be recorded. Mix: [`mixes/lights-on-nobody-home.flac`](artifacts/lights-on-nobody-home/mixes/lights-on-nobody-home.flac) (24-bit FLAC). |
| [Interstate Rain (rain-soaked jazz rock)](artifacts/interstate-rain/README.md) | A three-minute instrumental in E Dorian in the manner of a late-1960s rock band with a jazz rhythm section: a Rhodes-style electric piano and soft bass, a light sampled kit, one clean guitar into an echo, and a recorded rain and thunder. The sounds were fetched from freesound.org over MCP. Mix: [`mixes/interstate-rain-v1.flac`](artifacts/interstate-rain/mixes/interstate-rain-v1.flac) (24-bit FLAC). |
| [Street Hammer (hardcore techno)](artifacts/street-hammer/README.md) | Hardcore techno at 170 bpm in G minor, inspired by Orff's "Gassenhauer": a bouncing marimba figure under an original hoover tune, over a gabber kick, an offbeat distorted bass and a 909, with three heavy drops. Mix: [`mixes/street-hammer-v1.flac`](artifacts/street-hammer/mixes/street-hammer-v1.flac) (FLAC). Measured, not heard. |
