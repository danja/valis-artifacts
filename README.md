# valis-artifacts

Things made with [Valis](https://github.com/danja/valis), a standalone app and plugin that builds virtual analog instruments and effects from circuits described in RDF/Turtle.

Each piece here is a score played by Valis circuits, rendered to one stem per part and mixed. The MIDI files and the score script are kept, so the stems can be rebuilt and a piece finished in a DAW.

## Tracks

| Track | What it is |
|---|---|
| [All Fall Down](artifacts/all-fall-down/README.md) | A three-minute dance track at 140 bpm in A minor, built on nursery rhymes turned minor: "Twinkle, Twinkle" on a shimmering lead and then as a bass that says "baa", "London Bridge" falling in the break, "Mary Had a Little Lamb" on a toy piano. Eight new instruments, including a kick that falls like Humpty Dumpty. Mix: [`all-fall-down-v3.flac`](artifacts/all-fall-down/mixes/all-fall-down-v3.flac). Measured, not heard. |
| [Autumn Leaves](artifacts/autumn-leaves/README.md) | Five minutes of psychedelic acid rock on the chords of the standard: hypnotic drums, fuzz bass, a tonewheel organ through a rotating speaker, horn stabs and a fuzz lead, with room left for a guitar. Mix: [`autumn-leaves.flac`](artifacts/autumn-leaves/mixes/autumn-leaves.flac) (24-bit). |
| [Lights On, Nobody Home](artifacts/lights-on-nobody-home/README.md) | Latin punk for nylon guitars, guitarron, harp, trumpets and hand percussion. Bright mariachi verses in C major about people who aren't very bright, then a chorus that jumps into D Hungarian minor and turns punk. Lyrics and a note-by-note vocal guide are included, and the vocals are yet to be recorded. Mix: [`lights-on-nobody-home-v3.flac`](artifacts/lights-on-nobody-home/mixes/lights-on-nobody-home-v3.flac) (24-bit). |
| [Cumbia Cuántica](artifacts/cumbia-cuantica/README.md) | A simplified cumbia in A minor at 94 bpm with a gaita-style flute tune. Five circuits that each show off one element make it avant-garde: a Bassman-amp surf guitar, a fuzz and a diode buzz, a lo-fi codec ghost pad, a triplet arpeggio forced into A minor pentatonic, and a talk box that says nursery rhymes. Mix: [`cumbia-cuantica-v3.flac`](artifacts/cumbia-cuantica/mixes/cumbia-cuantica-v3.flac). Measured, not heard. |
| [Tide Table](artifacts/tide-table/README.md) | A slow instrumental in D minor at 78 bpm. A bowed cello plays the tune over harp arpeggios, bell droplets, a heartbeat kick and brushed maracas, and a pad run through a spectral codec carries the memory of it. Lyrics and a guide melody are included. Mix: [`tide-table-v1.flac`](artifacts/tide-table/mixes/tide-table-v1.flac). Measured, not heard. |
| [Interstate Rain](artifacts/interstate-rain/README.md) | A three-minute instrumental in E Dorian, in the manner of a late-1960s rock band with a jazz rhythm section: Rhodes-style piano, soft bass, a light sampled kit, a clean guitar into an echo and recorded rain and thunder fetched from freesound.org over MCP. Mix: [`interstate-rain-v1.flac`](artifacts/interstate-rain/mixes/interstate-rain-v1.flac) (24-bit). |
| [Street Hammer](artifacts/street-hammer/README.md) | Hardcore techno at 170 bpm in G minor, inspired by Orff's "Gassenhauer": a bouncing marimba figure under an original hoover tune, over a gabber kick, an offbeat distorted bass and a 909, with three heavy drops. Mix: [`street-hammer-v1.flac`](artifacts/street-hammer/mixes/street-hammer-v1.flac). Measured, not heard. |
| [Big Pickle](artifacts/big-pickle/README.md) | Driving techno at 138 bpm in A minor, 140 bars, for the year 2000's idea of the future: a slow bleep arpeggio wakes a machine, a supersaw pad and offbeat chord stabs open over a hard four-to-the-floor kick, and three drops land on a sub boom, with four risers. Five instruments written for it, including a 9-bit bleep, a five-saw lead and a noise-swept riser. Mix: [`big-pickle-v5.flac`](artifacts/big-pickle/mixes/big-pickle-v5.flac). Measured, not heard. |
| [Ilkley Allegretto](artifacts/ilkley-allegretto/README.md) | "On Ilkla Moor Baht 'at" over the rhythm of the slow movement of Beethoven's Seventh Symphony, 76 bpm in A major and A minor. Trumpets carry the tune over a cello and nylon guitar playing Beethoven, and in the finale the two play together. Mix: [`ilkley-allegretto-v1.flac`](artifacts/ilkley-allegretto/mixes/ilkley-allegretto-v1.flac). Measured, not heard. |
"Measured, not heard" means the mix was checked with measurements (loudness, peaks, spectrum) and not by listening.
